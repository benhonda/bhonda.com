This skill is the **methodology** half of analytics in an Adpharm app: how to decide
**what to track and why**, how to name and structure events, and how to model users,
accounts, and groups. It is distilled from [Accoil's product-tracking
playbook](https://github.com/Accoil/product-tracking-skills) (MIT © Accoil), retargeted to
how Adpharm builds.

**It is the companion to the `silo-analytics` skill, not a replacement.** Split of labour:

- **This skill — WHAT / WHY.** Tracking-plan design, event/property naming, the event
  taxonomy, B2B user/account/group modeling, events-vs-properties-vs-traits, snapshot
  metrics, anti-patterns. Reach for it on *"what should we track,"* *"design a tracking
  plan,"* *"is this a good event name,"* *"how do we model accounts,"* *"review our plan."*
- **`silo-analytics` — HOW.** Firing events, payload shape, `AnalyticsBrowser.load`,
  Time-on-Page, consent, server-side dedup. Reach for it on *"how do I fire this event."*

**Transport is a solved problem here.** The accoil base below sometimes assumes you build
your own delivery pipeline (RPC bridge, queue, backend routing). In an Adpharm app you do
**not** — `@adpharm/silo-analytics` already owns the proxy, batching, consent stamping, and
dedup. Read the base for its *modeling and naming* judgment; ignore its build-your-own-
transport mechanics and fire through silo-analytics instead.

Read the **Adpharm specifics** first (they win over the base), then use the reference index
